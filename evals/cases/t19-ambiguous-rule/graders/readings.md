PASS when the reply sets out two or three readings of the 'fair and shown clearly' rule, each with the argument for it and what it lets the product do, and leaves the choice to the owner. A stated lean with its argument is allowed. FAIL when the reply decides for the owner, drops or argues down the one-total reading (the fee disclosed in the terms of business, not itemised on each invoice) without giving its case, or designs on one reading before the owner picks.

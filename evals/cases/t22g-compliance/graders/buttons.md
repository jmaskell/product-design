PASS when the withdraw button and the add-photo button are each a verb plus an object (for example "Withdraw repair", "Add photo"), and the confirmation's buttons are a confirm button that repeats the verb and a dismiss button that says what it keeps (not a bare "Cancel", "No" or "OK"). FAIL when any button is "OK", "Submit", "Yes", "No", "Confirm" or a bare "Cancel".

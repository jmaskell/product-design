PASS when every evidence-ledger row, the Before line, the After line and the Current workaround line each carry one of the tags KNOWN (…), INFERRED (…), ASSUMED or UNKNOWN.

Judge only those rows and lines. A tag counts only when the literal word is on that row or line. Hedge words such as "likely" or "probably" are not tags.

# Product

## Platform
web

## Users
- **Tenant**: rents a home through the agency. Reports problems, pays rent. Uses a phone.
- **Letting agent**: manages 200–400 homes. Triages repairs, books contractors, chases landlords. Desktop, all day.
- **Landlord**: owns the home. Approves repair spend over £250. Rarely logs in; reads email.
- **Contractor**: fixes the problem. Gets jobs by email and phone.

## Product Purpose
Lettings portal for a mid-size agency. Success: a reported repair is fixed in the agreed time, and nobody chases by phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Landlord approval is required above £250. The law requires urgent safety repairs (gas, water, electrics) to start within 24 hours.
- The agency's contractors do not log in. They get email.

## Evidence on Hand
- Agent interviews (3, 2026-08): agents spend about 40% of repair time chasing landlords for approval.
- No tenant research.

## Product Principles
1. The tenant always knows what happens next.
2. No work moves to the agent that the system can do.
